## Overview
The power budget ensures that the allocated power sources can actually power the designed circuit. The budget goes through each component and lists their max current, as well as their chosen voltage source to calculate the maximum current flowing through the subsystem.

From the prepared power budget, the 5V rail will be sufficient power for this subsystem design. The fuse will help regulate the current and the circuit will remain functional.
